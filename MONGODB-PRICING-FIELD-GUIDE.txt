MongoDB Pricing Field Guide

Banners:
- startingPrice = category/listing "Price starts at" value.
- material = individual product calculator base rate per sq/ft (when that product uses a material base).

Example: blockout-fabric
- startingPrice: 6.75 => Banners category shows $6.75/sq ft.
- material: 10 => Blockout individual 24x24 (4 sq ft) calculates $40 wholesale before extras.

The active storefront pricing loader is pricing-v3.js. It loads /api/pricing from MongoDB first and uses local values only as fallback.
